The Frostmarch · Field manual

Build. Survive. Conquer.

You lead the teal Dawnclan against the red Ashclan. Destroy their stronghold before they destroy yours. The world is shared by your settlement and your warband.

1 · Command your warband

Tap a teal unit to select it. Tap a highlighted adjacent tile to move, or a highlighted enemy to attack. Every unit gets 2 action points (AP) each round. A newly recruited unit acts next round. Movement is orthogonal; shallow water is traversable.

2 · Grow your settlement

Move onto a tile next to your existing territory and choose Claim (1 AP, 1 gold). Select an owned tile to build a farm, lumber camp, mine or watchtower. Use Inspect to look at a tile without moving. Recruit at your stronghold or a free adjacent owned tile, up to 7 units.

3 · Use the terrain

Forests and watchtowers reduce damage; archers gain +1 attack damage from hills. Water and wet surfaces empower lightning. Oil ignites into fire. Frost freezes wet enemies and creates ice. Fire can melt ice. Lightning and fire surfaces can hurt allies, too.

4 · Prepare for winter

Each season lasts 3 rounds. Income and upkeep occur at the start of the next round, after the rival acts. Winter reduces food production and increases upkeep from 2 to 3 food per unit. A shortage deals 2 damage to every unit in that faction. Stockpile food and build farms before winter.

5 · Win the war

Attack the rival stronghold until its 28 HP reach zero. Units standing on a stronghold must be defeated before the stronghold itself can be hit. There is no time limit. Save progress locally or start a fresh skirmish at any time.
